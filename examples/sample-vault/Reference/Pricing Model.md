# Pricing Model

All prices are in EUR and exclude VAT. Invoices follow the [[Invoice Checklist]].

## Hourly
Standard rate is 90 per hour for design and 110 per hour for front-end work. Time is tracked in
15-minute blocks. Used for exploratory work such as the [[Brand Guidelines]].

## Fixed fee
Fixed fees are quoted from a scoped list of deliverables. The price is the estimated hours times the
hourly rate plus a 15 percent risk buffer. 50 percent is invoiced at kickoff and 50 percent at launch.
The [[Website Redesign]] uses this model.

### What a fixed fee includes
Two rounds of revisions per deliverable. A third round is billed hourly.

## Retainers
A monthly retainer reserves a number of hours. Unused hours expire after the month. Minimum term is
three months.

## Change requests
Anything outside the scoped deliverables is a change request. It is estimated in writing and approved
before work starts. Late copy changes after the freeze count as change requests.
