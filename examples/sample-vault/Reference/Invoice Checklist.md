# Invoice Checklist

Before sending an invoice, check each item. Pricing rules are in [[Pricing Model]].

- [ ] Client legal name and VAT number are correct
- [ ] Amounts match the signed quote
- [ ] Payment terms (net 30 by default) are stated
- [ ] The invoice number follows the sequence
